About

Where Kemetica Begins —
Your Gateway to Ancient Egypt

At Kemetica, we turn every journey into an unforgettable adventure. Founded in the heart of Aswan in 2018, we proudly serve travellers from around the world, crafting carefully designed expeditions that combine authenticity, comfort, and extraordinary value.

Our travellers are at the heart of everything we do. Your comfort, safety, and satisfaction are our top priorities. That's why we focus on delivering more than just trips — we create meaningful experiences filled with culture, beauty, and moments that last a lifetime.

From the moment you arrive, we take care of every detail. Our services include private transfers, carefully selected accommodations, expert local guides, and personalized itineraries. With 24/7 concierge support, your Kemetica journey is smooth, stress-free, and deeply personal.
